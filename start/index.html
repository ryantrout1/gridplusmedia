What happens after you send this

We read what you tell us and email you a link to pick a time for a setup call. On the call we talk through how your business runs, who your customers are and what you most want fixed, so we understand what you need.

When we agree on how to move forward, we pick a launch date and go over how reviews and changes work. After you pay, your first 90-day marketing plan arrives within a couple of days: every post, and exactly what goes out on each channel. You see the plan, the website and everything else, and you can ask for changes before it goes live.

We launch as quickly as we can, usually in less than a week. How fast depends on how quickly you review and send back your changes, since that part is yours. We also set up or clean up your website, your Google business profile and your social accounts so they all say the same thing. Once you are live, posts go out every week, reviews and comments get an answer in your voice, and every 90 days we look at what worked and plan the next quarter.
