# How to Get More Google Reviews

Google reviews shape whether a nearby customer picks up the phone or scrolls past to a competitor. If you are wondering how to get more of them, the good news is it usually comes down to a few habits you can start this week.

## Make It Easy to Leave a Review

Most customers are willing to leave a review, they just need a simple way to do it. Get your direct Google review link and put it where people already are: a text after a job is done, a line in a receipt email, or a small sign near checkout. The fewer steps between finishing up with you and leaving a review, the more reviews you'll see.

## Ask at the Right Moment

Timing matters more than the words you use. Ask right after a good experience, like when a customer thanks you in person or a job wraps up successfully. Waiting weeks means the moment has passed and the request feels random.

## Keep Your Listing Accurate

A mismatched address, wrong hours, or outdated phone number can quietly cost you reviews before a customer even walks in. If someone shows up to a locked door because your Google hours don't match your website, that frustration often turns into a negative review instead of a positive one. Keeping your hours, address and details the same across your Google profile, your website and other listings removes one of the easiest ways to lose trust.

## Reply to Every Review You Get

Replying to reviews, good and bad, shows future customers that someone is actually paying attention. A thoughtful reply in your own voice does more for your reputation than the star rating alone. This is also where many business owners get stuck, since writing a reply for every single review takes time they don't have.

## Never Buy or Fake Reviews

It can be tempting to shortcut the process, but invented reviews or testimonials put your listing at risk and erode trust the moment someone notices. The steadier path is consistent, honest requests paired with real replies over time.

## Make Reviews Part of a Bigger Routine, Not a One-Time Push

Asking for reviews once and then forgetting about it only gets you so far. Reviews add up when asking, replying and keeping your profile accurate becomes part of your regular routine instead of something you remember to do once in a while.
