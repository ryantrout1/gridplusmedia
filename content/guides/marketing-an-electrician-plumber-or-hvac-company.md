# Marketing an Electrician, Plumber or HVAC Company

Something breaks at the house and a person grabs their phone. They type "electrician near me" or "plumber near me" and pick whoever looks real and close. For a home service company, that moment matters a lot. This guide covers what to do about it, in the order that matters.

## Start with your Google profile

Check your profile with three questions. Does it say what you fix? Does it show your hours, including after hours? Does it name the area you serve? If a stranger can answer those three questions in a few seconds, you are easier to choose.

## Five things that make your listing easier to trust

None of these is fancy. Together they tell a stranger you are real, close and open.

1. **Your service area.** Say where you work, so nearby customers know you cover them. If customers do not come to your address, set a service area and hide the address.
2. **Your services, one by one.** Add each service separately, like drain repair, panel upgrades or AC tune ups.
3. **Real photos.** Finished jobs, your truck, your crew at work.
4. **Hours, including emergency hours.** Set regular hours. If you take emergency calls, say so.
5. **A reply to every review.** Thank people, answer concerns calmly, and keep going.

## Post the before and after

One post proves the work better than any ad: the before and after.

- Take a photo of the job when you arrive. Take another when you finish. Same angle if you can.
- Get the customer's okay first.
- Make sure no house numbers show, and nothing else private.
- Include the fix itself, the clean finished work, and a short line on what you did.

People hiring a tradesperson want to see that you do it right. A real photo says that better than words.

## Do you need Instagram?

Plumbers and other home pros often wonder. Honest answer: not first. Start with your Google profile, because that is where people look when a pipe is leaking. Then pick one place to show your work.

Instagram is good for photos of finished jobs, but only if you will keep it going. A page that stops after two posts does not help. If you will not post, skip it and put the effort into Google.

## Keep the details matching

Your hours, phone number and service area should read the same on Google, your website and any other listing. If you close early because a job is running long, change it everywhere the same day.
