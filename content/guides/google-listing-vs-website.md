# Website or Google Listing: Which Matters More?

It is one of the most common questions owners ask. The short answer is both, in that order: the listing first, the website second.

## Why the listing comes first

For many local searches, people see your Google listing before they ever reach a website. It is the box with your name, hours, photos and reviews that shows up in the results and on the map. If that box is wrong, some people never get as far as your site. They see the wrong hours, or a thin listing, and they call someone else.

## What your listing needs

- Correct hours, including holidays and any day you are closed.
- Your correct address, or your service area if you travel to customers.
- The right main category, plus any others that really apply.
- A clear description in your own words.
- Real photos of your place and your work.
- A phone number and website link that match what is on your site.

## What your website needs

Once the listing gets someone interested, your website has to back it up with the details a person needs to decide. For most local businesses that means:

- What you do, in plain words, with each service explained.
- Where you are or where you work.
- Your hours and how to reach you.
- A clear next step: call, book or ask a question.
- A few real photos, and the same facts your listing shows.

## They have to agree

A listing and a website that disagree cost you trust. If Google says you close at 7 and your site says 6, a customer shows up at a locked door, or decides not to come at all. Check that your hours, address and phone number match in both places. If you change something in one, change it in the other the same day.

## Where to start this week

If you only have time for one thing this week, fix the listing. Then work on the site. A good order for one afternoon:

1. Open your Google profile and check your hours, address, phone and categories.
2. Add a few real photos.
3. Compare it line by line with your website and fix any differences.
4. Then look at your site: does it say what you do and how to reach you, without making people hunt?
