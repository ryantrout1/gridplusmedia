## A marketing plan built around how electricians actually work

Electricians don't have time to sit down and write a Facebook post between a panel upgrade and an emergency call. Grid Pulse Media runs your marketing for you: your website, your Google Business Profile, your social accounts and your listings across directories like Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yelp. Everything is built from what you tell us on a setup call, so it's accurate and sounds like your business, and it all runs on a 90-day plan so you're never guessing what's happening or when.

## Who this is for

This service suits electrical contractors who want to keep showing up online without becoming their own marketing department. It fits well if:

- You haven't posted in months because the work keeps getting in the way
- Your hours or address don't match between Google, your website and other directories, and a customer has shown up when you were closed
- You paid an agency before and never knew what you were getting for the money
- You want reviews answered in your own voice instead of ignored

## What happens during setup

It starts with one conversation, the setup call. We learn about your business: the services you run, the areas you cover, your hours, and anything specific you want customers to know. From that conversation we map out your next 90 days, including what to promote, when, and on which channel. We then build or connect your website, set up your Google profile correctly, and get your social accounts in shape so everything is ready to run.

## What runs week to week

Once your 90-day plan is set, it runs without you having to remember it. Posts go out on schedule across your website, Google profile, Instagram and Facebook. Reviews and comments get replied to in your own voice, and we never quote or name a reviewer in any post. If something changes, like a job that's running long and you need to close early, you tell us once and it goes out everywhere, matching, so your website, Google profile and social accounts never contradict each other.

## What to expect after a change or update

When you tell us about a change, whether it's new hours, a closure, or a new service you've started offering, we update it across every channel at the same time. That way a customer checking Google sees the same thing as a customer checking your website or Facebook page. Posts and blog articles are also tied to your plan, so each one points somewhere, often to your blog or to the service page that explains what you offer, so interested readers have a clear next step.

## Every 90 days, we check and adjust

At the end of each 90-day cycle, we look at what worked over the last quarter and plan the next one with you. It's a repeating process: plan, do, check, adjust. That means your marketing keeps getting shaped around what actually fits your electrical business rather than running on autopilot forever.

Getting started takes one setup call. Tell us about your electrical business, your current website and social accounts if you have them, and the one thing you most want fixed, and we'll take it from there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to write my own posts or blog articles?

No. Everything starts from what you tell us on the setup call, and from there we write every post and article, make the images, and publish it all on schedule across your website, Google profile and social accounts. You don't need to come up with ideas or sit down to write anything yourself.

### Will my hours and address be the same everywhere?

Yes, keeping everything matching is a core part of the plan. Your hours, address and other details are kept consistent across your website, Google Business Profile, social accounts and directories like Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yelp, so customers never show up expecting something different from what they find.

### What happens if I have a last minute closure or schedule change?

You tell us once and it goes out everywhere, matching. Whether it's a job running long, a staff training day, or any other change to your hours, we update your website, Google profile and social accounts together so the information customers see is always the same.

### Do you reply to reviews for my electrical business?

Yes, replying to reviews and comments is part of the plan, and replies are written in your business's own voice. We never quote or name a reviewer in any post, and we don't write or invent reviews on your behalf.

### How often do posts go out?

Posting happens on a schedule set as part of your 90-day plan, which is mapped out after your setup call based on what fits your electrical business. Every 90 days we check what worked and plan the next quarter, adjusting the schedule and content as needed.

### What is the 90-day plan and how does it start?

The 90-day plan is the ongoing cycle Grid Pulse Media runs for your marketing: website, Google profile, social accounts and listings, all kept matching and posted on schedule. It starts with a single setup call where we learn about your electrical business, and after that it runs without you needing to manage it week to week.
