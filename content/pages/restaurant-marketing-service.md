## What this service covers

Running a restaurant means your attention belongs in the kitchen and dining room, not in five different apps trying to remember if you updated your holiday hours on Google. Our restaurant marketing service takes that job off your plate. We handle your website, your Google profile, your social accounts and your listings, all built from what you actually tell us, and all kept saying the same thing.

No guessing about what to post. No stale profile from last spring. Just your real hours, your real specials and your real voice, going out on a schedule.

## Who this is for

This fits restaurant owners who know their marketing has gone quiet but don't have a spare hour to fix it. It also fits owners who hired an agency before, paid every month, and never knew what that money actually bought. And it fits anyone whose hours on Google don't match the website, the kind of mismatch that sends a hungry customer to a locked door on a night you're actually open.

If you want your restaurant to keep showing up online without becoming your second job, this is built for that.

## What happens when we start working together

It begins with a setup call. We ask about your restaurant: your hours, your menu highlights, your closures, what makes your place yours. After you start, we use that conversation to build a 90-day plan, deciding what to promote and where, whether that's a weekend special, a new dish or a holiday closure notice.

From there we build or clean up what's needed: your website, your Google business profile and your social accounts, set up correctly and matching each other. Once that foundation is in place, the weekly work starts. Posts go out on the schedule your plan sets. Reviews and comments get answered in your own voice. If something changes, like a one-off closure for staff training, you tell us once and it goes out everywhere: your Google profile, your website, Instagram and Facebook, all updated together.

## What to expect after setup

Once your 90-day plan is running, you shouldn't need to think about posting again. Each post is built from what you told us, so it sounds like your restaurant, not a template. Posts are also built to send people somewhere, often a blog article on your own site that points toward a service page, like your menu or your booking page, so there's always a next step for someone who just saw your post.

Your listings, including places like Apple Maps, Bing Places and Foursquare, are checked so your hours and address stay consistent everywhere a hungry customer might look. Every 90 days we check what worked, make adjustments, and plan the next quarter together.

## How to book

Getting started takes one conversation. Answer a few quick questions about your restaurant, and we'll follow up to schedule your setup call. You can [get started here](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) whenever you're ready.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to come up with what to post each week?

No. Every post starts from what you tell us about your restaurant during the setup call and in ongoing check-ins, so you don't have to brainstorm ideas yourself. We write the posts, make the images and publish them on the schedule set in your 90-day plan, across your website, Google profile and social accounts.

### What if my hours change or I need to close for a day?

You tell us once, and it goes out everywhere. Whether it's a one-time closure for staff training or a permanent change in hours, we update your website, Google profile and social accounts together so nothing is left out of date and no customer shows up to a locked door.

### Will someone reply to my Google and social media reviews?

Yes. Replies are written in your restaurant's own voice, based on what you've told us about how you talk to customers. Reviewers are never named or quoted in posts, keeping replies professional while still sounding like they genuinely came from you.

### How does this keep my listings consistent?

As part of the service, your details are kept matching across listings such as Google, Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and others. If something changes, like your address or hours, it gets updated across these listings so customers see the same accurate information wherever they search.

### How do I get started with this service?

Getting started begins with a short form that takes about five minutes, covering basics like your business name, website and social accounts. After we review it, we'll email you a link to pick a time for your setup call, where we learn about your restaurant. After you start, we build your first 90-day plan.

### Will my posts actually bring people to my website or menu?

Posts are built with a next step in mind. A post on Instagram, Facebook or Google gives people a reason to click through to an article on your own blog, which then points to a specific page, like your menu or booking page, so there's always somewhere for an interested customer to go next.
