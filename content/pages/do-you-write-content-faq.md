Short answer: yes. You don't have to come up with ideas, write posts, or sit down and draft anything. Everything we publish starts from what you tell us about your business, so it stays accurate and sounds like you, not a template.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to write anything myself?

No. You tell us about your business once, on a setup call, and we take it from there. We come up with what to say, write the posts and articles, make the images, and get it all scheduled. You review and approve, but you're not stuck writing captions or blog drafts.

### How do you know what to say if I don't write it?

Everything starts from what you actually tell us, your services, your hours, what makes your business different, and anything you want promoted. We use that to write posts and articles in your voice, so it sounds like you and stays accurate, not generic filler that could describe any business.

### Will the content sound like a generic template?

It shouldn't, because it's built from your own details rather than stock phrases. Every post starts from what you told us about your business, so the language reflects how you actually describe your work, not a one size fits all script.

### Who decides what topics get covered?

That's part of your 90-day plan, which we map out together on the setup call. We decide what to promote and when, so each post and article has a purpose, whether that's a service, a seasonal offer, or something new happening at your business.

### Do you also write replies to reviews and comments?

Yes. We reply to reviews and comments in your business's own voice, based on what you've told us, so responses sound consistent with everything else we post for you.

### What if something changes, like my hours or a closure?

You tell us once, and we update it everywhere, your website, Google profile and social accounts, so the details match. We never state hours or specials that differ from what you've reported, so customers always see accurate information.

### Can I review posts before they go out?

Your 90-day plan lays out what's being promoted and when, and the setup call is where you shape the direction. If you want to see specific posts before they publish, that's something to raise when you get started so we can set up the right workflow for you.

### How do I get started with this?

You can get started by filling out a short form that takes about five minutes, covering your business basics, website, Google profile and social accounts. We review it and send you a link to pick a time for your setup call. Visit https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/ to begin.
