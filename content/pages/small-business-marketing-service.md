## What this service is

Grid Pulse Media runs your marketing for you, every week, so your business keeps showing up online without you having to think up a post or remember to log in anywhere. We handle your website, your Google profile, your social accounts, your blog articles and your listings across the directories customers check, all from one monthly service instead of several agencies or freelancers.

Everything starts from what you actually tell us about your business. We write every post and article in your voice, make the images, and publish on schedule. When something changes, like your hours or a closure, you tell us once and it goes out everywhere it needs to, matching.

## Who this suits

This service fits owners who are too busy running the business to also run its marketing. It also fits owners who've paid an agency before and never knew what they got for it, and owners whose hours or address don't match between Google, their website and other listings, which sends customers to a locked door.

If you want your business to keep showing up consistently, without personally writing posts or checking that every listing still matches, this is built for that.

## What happens when you start

It begins with one conversation. On a setup call, we learn your business: what you offer, your hours, your tone, what makes you different. From that, we map out a 90-day plan that decides what to promote, when, and where it should send people.

From there we build or connect what's needed: a website that looks like your business rather than a template, your Google profile set up correctly, and your social accounts in order. Posts then go out every week on the schedule from your plan, reviews get answered in your voice, and your details are checked across Google, your website, your social accounts and listings like Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yellow Pages, so they stay the same everywhere.

Each post is built to send someone further: a social post catches attention, it leads to a blog article on your own site written for that moment, the article points to the service page that explains what you offer, and booking is one tap away from there.

## What to expect after setup

Once the setup call and 90-day plan are done, the service runs without you needing to manage it week to week. Posts, articles and listing checks happen on schedule. Every 90 days, we look at what worked, adjust, and plan the next quarter, so the plan keeps improving cycle after cycle instead of going stale.

If anything about your business changes in the meantime, hours, a closure, a new special, you just tell us once and we update it everywhere it appears.

## How to book

Getting started takes one short questionnaire, about five minutes, covering your business basics and any accounts you already have. We review it and send you a link to pick a time for your setup call.

Ready to stop remembering to post? [Get started](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) and we'll set up your 90-day plan.
