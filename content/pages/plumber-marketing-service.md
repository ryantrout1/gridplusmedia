## What this service is

Grid Pulse Media runs ongoing marketing for plumbing businesses so you don't have to stop mid-job to think up a post or fix a wrong phone number online. We handle your website, your Google business profile, your social accounts and your listings across directories like Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yellow Pages, all kept saying the same thing. Everything runs on a 90-day plan built around your actual business, not a generic template.

## Who this suits

This is built for plumbing business owners who are out on calls all day and don't have time to post online or check that their hours match everywhere. It also suits owners who tried an agency before and never knew what they were paying for each month, and owners whose Google hours, website and directory listings don't match, which sends customers to a locked door or an empty shop at the wrong time.

## What happens in a setup

It starts with one conversation. We learn your business: what you do, who you serve, what's busy season, what sets you apart. After you start, we use that call to map out the next 90 days, deciding what to promote and when. Then we build the pieces: a website that reflects your actual business rather than a stock design, your Google profile set up correctly, and your social accounts in order. Once that's in place, the weekly work starts. Posts go out, reviews get replies in your own voice, and your details stay consistent everywhere customers might look.

Anything you tell us, like a closure for staff training or a schedule change, gets sent out across your Google profile, website and social accounts so they all match. We never post hours, closures or specials you haven't told us.

## What to expect after

Once the 90-day plan is running, posts are written and scheduled for you using what you've told us about your business, so they sound like you rather than a copywriter. Each post is built to lead somewhere: to a blog article on your own site, which then points to the service page that explains what you offer, with booking one tap away. Reviews and comments get answered in your voice, never quoting or naming the reviewer. Every 90 days we check what worked, adjust, and plan the next quarter, so the plan keeps moving instead of sitting still.

## How to book

Getting started takes one short questionnaire, about five minutes, covering your business basics and any current website, Google profile or social accounts. After that, we review it and email you a link to pick a setup call time.

Ready to stop chasing posts and mismatched listings? [Get started](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) and we'll take it from there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you write the posts for my plumbing business or do I have to come up with ideas?

We write every post, article and caption ourselves, based on what you tell us about your plumbing business during the setup call and ongoing check-ins. You never have to think up a post idea or remember to publish anything. Your 90-day plan decides what gets posted and when, built around your actual services and schedule, not a generic template used for every business.

### Can you fix my Google hours if they don't match my website?

Yes, keeping your hours, address and other details matching across your Google profile, website and directory listings is a core part of this service. If something changes, like a closure or a schedule update, you tell us once and it goes out everywhere it needs to, so customers aren't left showing up to a locked door at the wrong time.

### Will you reply to reviews for my business?

Yes, we reply to reviews and comments in your own voice, based on what you've told us about how you talk to customers. We never quote or name a reviewer in any post or reply, keeping things professional and consistent with how your business actually communicates.

### What happens during the first 90-day plan?

It starts with a setup call where we learn your plumbing business. After you start, we map out what to promote over the next 90 days and where, whether that's your Google profile, website blog, or social accounts. We build or adjust your website and accounts, then posts and updates run weekly from there. At the end of 90 days we review what worked and plan the next cycle.

### Do I need to already have a website and social accounts?

No, you can start with what you have, even if that's nothing yet. The getting started questionnaire asks for your website, Google profile and social accounts as optional fields, so you can skip anything you don't have. We'll set up what's missing as part of your initial plan.

### How much does this cost?

Pricing details are covered on our pricing page rather than here, since it depends on your setup and plan. The best next step is to fill out the short questionnaire to get started, and we'll follow up with specifics for your business.
