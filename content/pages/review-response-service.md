## What this service is

Reviews come in whether you're watching for them or not, and every one left unanswered is a missed chance to sound like yourself in public. Our review response service means someone on our team reads every new review on your Google profile and other listings, then writes a reply that sounds like you, not like a template. We start from what you've told us about your business and how you talk to customers, so the tone is consistent whether the review is glowing or a little frustrated.

## Who this suits

This is for owners who know reviews matter but don't have time to check five different sites every day. It's also for anyone who's been burned before, maybe by a reply that went out in a voice that didn't sound like the business at all, or by months where nothing got answered because it fell through the cracks. If your listings already have mismatched details or inconsistent replies, this service folds into the same system that keeps your hours, address and other facts matching everywhere.

## What happens during setup

It starts with the setup call that kicks off your 90-day plan. We ask about how you'd naturally respond to a compliment, a complaint or a question left in a review, and what details about your business should never be left out, like a specific service or a policy. From there we have enough to write replies that sound like you wrote them yourself, even on the days you're too busy to think about it.

## What happens day to day

Once the plan is running, new reviews get checked and answered on a regular schedule rather than piling up. We never name or quote a reviewer in any public post or reply, and we never invent details about what happened, every response starts from what you've actually told us about the business. If a review raises something we're not sure how to answer, we check with you before it goes out, so nothing gets said that doesn't match how you'd actually handle it.

## What to expect after

You'll see replies going out across your Google profile and the other listings we're keeping in sync, written in a consistent voice. As part of the regular check-in every 90 days, we look at what's been coming in and adjust how we're responding if your business or your preferences have changed. This sits alongside the rest of your marketing, so a review reply can also point people toward a blog article or service page when it makes sense.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you respond to every single review, even small ones?

Yes, every new review that comes in gets checked and answered as part of your ongoing plan, not just the ones that seem important. Keeping up with all of them, good and bad, is part of what makes the replies feel consistent instead of random. If a review needs your input before we respond, we'll check with you first so the reply still sounds right coming from your business.

### Will the replies actually sound like me?

That's the goal. Everything starts from what you tell us on the setup call about how you talk to customers and what matters to your business, so the reply is built from your own words and preferences rather than a generic script. If something doesn't sound right, we adjust it, because the whole point is that it reads like you wrote it.

### Do you ever name or quote the person who left the review?

No, we never name or quote a reviewer in any reply or post, that's a firm rule we follow for every business we work with. Replies address what was said without repeating identifying details back in public, which keeps things professional and protects everyone's privacy.

### What if a review is negative or unfair?

We reply to negative reviews the same way we reply to positive ones, starting from how you'd actually want to handle it. We never invent an excuse or a promise about what will happen next, we stick to what you've told us is true about the business and how you handle situations like it.

### Is this a separate service or part of something bigger?

Review responses are one piece of the full 90-day marketing plan we set up together, alongside your website, Google profile, social posts and listings. It's not sold as a standalone add-on, it's built into the same system so your reviews stay consistent with everything else customers see about your business.

### How do you know what to say if I haven't told you about a situation?

If a review mentions something we don't have context on, we check with you before replying rather than guessing. We only ever work from what you've actually shared with us, so we won't invent a detail or a reason just to fill out a response.
