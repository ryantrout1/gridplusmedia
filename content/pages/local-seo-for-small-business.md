## What this service is

Local search engine optimization, often shortened to local SEO, is the work of making sure your business shows up correctly when someone nearby searches for what you do. That means your Google profile, your website and the directories customers check all say the same thing about your hours, address and services. At Grid Pulse Media, we handle this as part of keeping your whole online presence consistent and current, not as a one-time fix.

A lot of local SEO problems aren't really about rankings. They're about mismatches: your website says you close at 6, your Google profile says 7, and a customer shows up to a locked door. We start by fixing that, then keep it that way going forward.

## Who this suits

This is for local business owners who want to be found by people searching nearby, but don't have time to manage a Google profile, check listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yellow Pages, or write content aimed at local search. It also suits owners who've noticed their hours or address don't match everywhere, and owners who paid an agency before and never knew what was actually done each month.

If you're opening a new location, changing hours, or just tired of customers calling to ask if you're open, this service is built around exactly that.

## What happens during setup

It starts with one setup call. We learn your business directly from you: what you offer, your real hours, your address, what makes you different. Once you start, we use that conversation to map out your 90-day plan, which decides what gets promoted, when, and where.

As part of setup, we check your website, your Google profile, and your social accounts, and line them up with what you told us. We also work through listings like Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yellow Pages so they match too. Anything that was out of sync gets corrected at this stage.

## What happens after setup

Once the initial setup is done, local SEO isn't a one-time task we file away. It keeps running. When something changes, like a holiday closure or new hours, you tell us once, and it goes out everywhere: your Google profile, your website, and your social accounts, all updated to match.

We also keep writing posts and blog articles tied to your 90-day plan, so there's fresh, accurate content for search engines to find, each one pointing back to the service page that explains what you offer. Reviews and comments get replies in your own voice, which also plays into how local search sees your business.

Every 90 days, we check what worked, adjust, and plan the next quarter, so your local presence keeps getting more accurate and complete over time instead of going stale.
