Here are answers to the questions owners ask most when they're figuring out what it costs to keep their marketing running. If you don't see yours, reach out through [Get started](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) and ask us directly before you book a call.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Grid Pulse Media cost?

It is $349 per month plus a one-time setup fee of $699. That one monthly price covers your website, your Google profile and your social posts, planned, written and kept current. You can start by filling out the Get started form, which takes about five minutes.

### Is this one price instead of paying separate people for each thing?

Yes. Instead of hiring someone for your website, someone else for social posts, and another person to manage your Google profile and listings, Grid Pulse Media handles all of it under one monthly plan. That is the whole idea: one point of contact, one bill, and everything kept matching across your site, your Google profile, and your social accounts.

### What exactly am I paying for each month?

You are paying for your 90-day plan to run: posts written and published on schedule, your Google profile and listings kept matching your website, reviews and comments answered in your own voice, and blog articles that connect back to your services. Each week we check how your pages are doing in search and make one change at a time. Every 90 days we check what worked and plan the next quarter, so the work keeps adjusting instead of staying static.

### Will I know what I am getting for my money each month?

Yes. A common complaint we hear is paying an agency before and never really knowing what they did each month. We build around a 90-day plan you see upfront, so you know what is being posted, where, and why, rather than wondering what happened to your payment.

### Is there a setup fee on top of the monthly cost?

Yes, a one-time setup fee of $699. Setup covers building or cleaning up your website, setting up your Google profile and social accounts correctly, and mapping your first 90-day plan.

### Can I cancel if it is not working for me?

There is a three-month minimum, and after that it is month to month. Your website is yours to keep either way. We go over the terms on your setup call so you know exactly what you are signing up for before anything starts.

### Do I need a website already, or does that cost extra?

You do not need one already. If you do not have a website, building one that looks like your business, not a generic template, is part of your setup. If you skip the website field when you start, that is fine too. We will cover it when we talk.
