## What this service is

This is website design built for local businesses that want their site to actually do something, not just sit there looking nice. We start with a setup call where we learn your business in your own words: what you offer, who you serve, and what makes you different. From that conversation we build a website that looks like you, not a template pulled off a shelf, and we set it up so it fits into your ongoing 90-day marketing plan.

Your site isn't a one-time project that gets handed off and forgotten. It's the home base that your Google profile, Instagram, Facebook and blog posts all point back to, so a post that catches someone's eye leads somewhere real.

## Who this is for

This service suits local business owners who don't have the time or the inclination to build and maintain a website themselves. It's a good fit if you're opening for the first time and need a site that matches everything else, or if your current site is out of date and doesn't match your Google profile or social accounts anymore. It also works well for owners who've been burned before: paid someone to build a site, then never heard from them again and had no idea what they were actually getting each month.

## What happens in a visit

It starts with a setup call. We ask about your business: your hours, your address, what you sell, what you want customers to do when they land on the page. Nothing goes on the site that you didn't tell us first. From there we map out your 90-day plan, which decides what pages get built first, what the site should promote, and how it connects to your blog and social posts.

We then build the site itself, matching your Google profile, your social accounts and your actual hours and address, so a customer never shows up to a locked door because your website said something different from Google. Every page is written from what you told us, so it sounds like your business, not boilerplate.

## What to expect after

Once the site is live, it doesn't sit still. It's checked regularly against your Google profile, Instagram, Facebook and other listings to make sure everything still matches. When something changes on your end, like a closure or new hours, you tell us once and it goes out everywhere, including your website. Blog articles and service pages get added as part of your 90-day plan, each one written to send visitors toward a clear next step, usually booking or getting in touch.

You'll also get a plain one-page report so you always know what's been done, instead of wondering what you're paying for.

## How to book

Getting started takes about five minutes. You answer a few questions about your business, your current website if you have one, and the one thing you most want fixed. We review it and email you a link to pick a time for your setup call. From there, the 90-day plan takes shape and your site becomes part of it.

Ready to get your website working for you? [Get started](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) and we'll be in touch to schedule your setup call.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an existing website to get started?

No. If you already have a site, you can share the link during setup and we'll work from there. If you don't have one yet, that's fine too. The setup form simply asks you to skip that question, and we'll build from scratch based on what you tell us about your business during the setup call.

### Will the website match my Google profile and social accounts?

Yes. Keeping everything matching is part of the service. Your hours, address and other details are set up the same way across your website, Google profile, Instagram, Facebook and other listings customers check, so there's no mismatch that sends someone to a locked door or an old address.

### How long does it take to get a website live?

It starts with a setup call where we learn your business and map out your 90-day plan, which shapes what gets built and when. Because every site is built from your own details rather than a template, timing depends on your plan, but the process always begins with that conversation so nothing goes live that doesn't match what you told us.

### Who writes the content on my website?

We do, starting from what you tell us in the setup call and afterward. Every page, post and article is built from your own words so it sounds like your business and stays accurate, rather than generic text that could belong to anyone.

### What happens if my hours or address change later?

You tell us once, and the update goes out everywhere, including your website, Google profile and social accounts. This keeps everything consistent so customers always see the same hours and address no matter where they look you up.

### Is the website a one-time project or ongoing?

It's ongoing. Your website is part of your 90-day marketing plan, which gets checked and adjusted regularly. Instead of building a site once and walking away, we keep it tied to your blog, social posts and listings so it keeps working as your business changes.
