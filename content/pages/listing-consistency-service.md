## What this service is

Customers check more places than just your website before they visit. They look at your Google profile, Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare, Yellow Pages and more. If your hours or address don't match across all of them, someone ends up at your door when you're closed, or they give up and call a competitor instead.

Our listing consistency service keeps those details the same everywhere. You tell us once what's changed, a holiday closure, new hours, a staff training day, and we update it across your Google profile, your website and the directories customers actually check. We also keep checking afterward to make sure nothing has drifted out of sync.

## Who this is for

This service suits business owners who have noticed their listings don't quite match, maybe the website says one set of hours and Google says another. It's also a fit if you've never actually checked how your business looks across all those different platforms and suspect there might be gaps. If you're tired of being the one who has to remember to update five different places every time something changes, this is built for that exact problem.

## What happens when you tell us something changed

You send us the update once, in plain language. Something like "closed Monday, Oct 13, staff training day, back Tuesday at 9." From there, we take that single piece of information and push it out everywhere it needs to appear: your Google profile, your website, and the other listings tied to your business. You don't have to log into each platform separately or try to remember where you've posted hours before.

After the update goes out, we check that everything still lines up. That means confirming your Google profile, your website and your other listings all say the same thing, not three slightly different versions of the truth.

## What to expect after setup

Once your listings are set up and matching, the work doesn't stop there. As part of your ongoing 90-day plan, we keep watching for anything that's fallen out of sync and keep your details current as things change at your business. This sits alongside the other parts of your plan, your website, your Google profile, your social posts and your blog, so everything customers see about your business tells the same story.

If you ever notice something that doesn't match, you can flag it and we'll get it corrected as part of the ongoing service.
