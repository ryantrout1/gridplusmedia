## What this service is

This is the ongoing posting and publishing side of Grid Pulse Media's work: your website, your Google profile, and your social accounts all kept current from a single plan. Instead of you writing posts, remembering to log in, or hiring separate help for each channel, we handle the writing, the images, and the publishing on a schedule, so your business keeps showing up without you having to think about it week to week.

Everything starts from what you actually tell us about the business, so what goes out sounds like you and matches what's true, not a generic template.

## Who this is for

This fits owners who know they should be posting regularly but don't have the time or the habit for it. It also fits owners who've been burned before: paid an agency and never knew what they were actually getting each month. And it fits anyone whose hours, address or details have drifted out of sync across Google, the website and other listings, the kind of mismatch that sends a customer to a locked door.

If you're the one juggling the website, the Google profile, Instagram and Facebook on top of actually running the business, this is built to take that off your plate.

## What happens in a setup

It starts with one conversation. We learn the business: what you do, who you serve, what's changing, what you want more of. From that conversation we build a 90-day plan that maps out what gets promoted, when, and on which channel.

From there we set up or clean up what needs it: a website that looks like your business rather than a template, your Google profile set up correctly, and your social accounts in order. Once that's in place, the plan starts running: posts go out on schedule, reviews get replies in your own voice, and your details are checked across channels so your hours and address say the same thing everywhere, including places like Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yellow Pages.

## What to expect after setup

Once the plan is running, it keeps running without you needing to chase it. Posts publish on schedule across your website, Google profile and social accounts, each one tied to the current 90-day plan so it has somewhere to send people, often your blog or a service page, with booking one tap away from there.

If something changes on your end, like a closure or new hours, you tell us once and it goes out everywhere it needs to, matching. Every 90 days, we check what worked, adjust, and plan the next quarter, so the approach keeps getting sharper over time rather than staying static.

## How to book

Getting started takes one short form, about five minutes, covering your business basics and whatever accounts you already have. We review it and email you a link to pick a time for the setup call.

If you're ready to stop remembering to post and start a 90-day plan that keeps your website, Google profile and social accounts showing up and matching, [get started here](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to write the posts myself?

No. Every post starts from what you tell us about your business in the setup conversation, and we write it, make the image, and publish it on schedule. You're not expected to come up with ideas or sit down to write anything yourself. The goal is for it to sound like you without taking your time.

### Which channels do you post to?

Posting covers your Google profile, your website, and your social accounts, including Instagram and Facebook, with more channels being added. Beyond posting, we also check that your details, like hours and address, stay matching across listings such as Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yellow Pages.

### What if my hours or details change?

You tell us once and it goes out everywhere it needs to, with your website, Google profile and social accounts all updated to match. This is meant to prevent the common problem of a customer showing up to find hours or details that don't line up with what's posted online.

### How often does the plan get reviewed?

Every 90 days we check what's worked, adjust the approach, and plan the next quarter. This keeps the posting schedule and the channels being used tied to what's actually happening in your business rather than running on autopilot indefinitely.

### Will you reply to reviews and comments too?

Yes, replies to reviews and comments are handled in your business's own voice as part of the ongoing service. We don't quote or name individual reviewers in posts, and nothing we publish promises a specific result.

### How do I get started?

You fill out a short form, about five minutes, covering your business name, contact details, and whatever accounts you already have, like your website or Google profile. We review it and email you a link to pick a time for a setup call. Once you start, we build your first 90-day plan.
