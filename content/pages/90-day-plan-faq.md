Here are answers to common questions about what our 90-day plan actually covers. It starts with a setup call where we learn your business, then we build and run your marketing so you don't have to think about it week to week. If you don't see your question answered here, reach out and we'll walk you through it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is included in the 90-day plan?

The plan covers your website, your Google profile, your social accounts like Instagram and Facebook, and your listings on sites like Apple Maps, Bing Places, Foursquare and Yelp. We write and publish posts and blog articles on schedule, keep your hours, address and details matching everywhere, and reply to reviews and comments in your voice. Every 90 days we check what worked and plan the next quarter.

### How do you know what to post about?

It starts with you. On the setup call we learn about your business, and everything we write going forward starts from what you've told us, so it's accurate and sounds like you. When something changes, like a closure or new hours, you tell us once and it goes out everywhere, matching.

### Will my website, Google profile and social accounts all say the same thing?

Yes. Keeping everything matching is a core part of the plan. If your hours change or you have a one-off closure, we update your website, Google profile and social accounts together so customers never show up expecting something different from what they find.

### Do you reply to reviews and comments for me?

Yes, replying to reviews and comments is part of the plan, and we write replies in your business's own voice. We never quote or name a reviewer in any post, and we don't write or invent reviews on your behalf.

### What happens after the first 90 days?

Every 90 days we check what worked over the last quarter, adjust based on that, and plan the next one with you. It's a repeating cycle of planning, doing, checking and adjusting, so the plan keeps getting better suited to your business over time.

### Do I need to come up with ideas or write anything myself?

No. We come up with what to say, write every post and article, make the images, and publish everything on schedule across your website, Google profile and social accounts. You tell us about your business and any changes, like a closure, and we take it from there.

### How much does the 90-day plan cost?

Pricing details are on our pricing page, so take a look there for current information. If you want a clearer picture based on your specific business, the best next step is to start the setup process and we can go over it with you directly.

### How do I get started?

You can get started by filling out a short form on our site, answering a few questions about your business, your website and your social accounts. It takes about five minutes. We'll review what you send and email you a link to pick a time for your setup call.
