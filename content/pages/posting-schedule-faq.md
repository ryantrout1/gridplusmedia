Owners often ask how often we'll actually post once we're handling things. Here's how our schedule works, based on the 90-day plan we build together at setup, so you always know what's happening and when.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often will you post on my social media

Posting frequency is set during your 90-day plan, built around what fits your business and what you told us at setup. Instead of posting at random, we map out what to promote and when across the next 90 days, then publish on that schedule every week. You won't have to think up an idea or remember to post, it just keeps running in the background.

### Will the posts actually sound like my business

Yes. Every post starts from what you told us, so it's accurate and reads like you, not generic filler. We don't invent details about your business. If something changes, like hours or a closure, you tell us once and it updates everywhere it needs to.

### Will my social media, website and Google profile all say the same thing

That's the point of the whole system. When you tell us something changes, like an hour, a closure or a special, it goes out everywhere at once: your Google profile, your website, Instagram and Facebook. We then check that everything still matches, so customers never show up expecting something different than what's posted.

### Do you reply to comments and reviews too

Yes, we reply to every review and comment in your business's own voice, keeping the tone consistent with your posts and your site. We never quote or name a reviewer, and we don't invent reviews or testimonials, replies are grounded in what actually happened.

### What happens after the first 90 days

Every 90 days we check what worked, adjust, and plan the next quarter together. That means your posting schedule isn't set once and forgotten, it gets reviewed and refined on a regular cycle so it keeps fitting your business as things change.

### Do posts just sit on social media or do they send people somewhere

Each post is tied to your 90-day plan, so it has somewhere to send people, often your blog or a specific service page. A post catches attention on Instagram, Facebook or Google, leads to an article on your own site, and that article points to the service page, with booking just one tap away.

### Can I see what you post each month

You'll know what's happening because everything follows the 90-day plan we build with you at setup, covering what gets promoted and when.
