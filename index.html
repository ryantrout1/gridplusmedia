Questions

Do I have to do anything?

A little. Tell us when something changes, like new hours or an event, and read the plan before it starts. We handle the rest.

What if something is wrong?

Tell us once and we fix it everywhere. We also check every week that your details match.

Will the posts sound like me?

Yes. We learn how you talk about your business on the setup call. You see every post ahead of time and can change it before it goes out.

Do I keep my accounts?

Yes. Your Google profile and social accounts stay yours. We only get access to run them, and your website is yours to keep.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Start with three months. After that it's month to month and you can cancel any time. If you leave, you keep your website and your accounts.
